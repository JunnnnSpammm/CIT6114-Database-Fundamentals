# Lab 5 Guide: books createDB, join & ERD – weak & strong

## Part A1: Discussion

Draw a conceptual E-R Diagram based on the following business rules: 
1.	The production company has several film crews.
2.	Each film crew is comprised of at least 15 staff members and each staff member only works with one (1) film crew.
3.	Each film crew works on several projects at the same time but no more than four (4) projects at any time.
4.	Each project is headed by one (1) director which directs and oversees the rest of the crew operations.
5.	Any director can only handle one (1) project at any time.
6.	For large film projects, up to two (2) crews can be cooperating and handle by one (1) director.
